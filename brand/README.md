# brand/

The MarshWorks "made by" block (`marshworks-made-by.html`) and its styles
(`marshworks-made-by.css`), copied as they came from the MarshWorks brand kit
by `sync-brand.mjs`. Don't edit them here; rerun the sync after the kit changes.

The MarshWorks name and logo are not covered by RadioGrid's GPL license (see
the License section of the README). A fork is welcome; give it its own name
and mark.
