# Contributing to RadioGrid

Thanks for wanting to help. Bug reports and ideas are welcome as GitHub
issues.

3. confirm the contribution is your own work, or that you have the right

If you can't agree to that, please open an issue describing the change
instead, and we'll write it ourselves.

## Names and logos

The RadioGrid, RadioGridXL and MarshWorks names and logos aren't covered by
the GPL. A fork is welcome; give it its own name and mark.
