Screen direction

Allow pinch to zoom

Sound and controls

Buttons while playing
Volume
Use the side buttons. Apple doesn't let web apps set the volume.
🔈 100%
The loudest the app plays. The device's volume buttons still work below it.
Voice announcements
Says "Now playing…", "Paused" and "Stopped" aloud.
Audio tones
A short rising tone when a station starts, and a falling one when it pauses.
Auto-resume
When the app opens, plays the station that was playing when it closed. Never after a Stop.
Haptic feedback
Vibrates briefly on button taps.
